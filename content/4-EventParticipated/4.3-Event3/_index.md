# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day 30/05/2026”

### Event Objectives

- Empower students to deliver presentations with confidence and practice their spoken English
- Facilitate an authentic environment where both students and working professionals can exchange tech insights
- Challenge attendees to break out of their comfort zones and expand their professional networks

### Speakers

- **Huỳnh Thái Linh** – Demonstrated the process of integrating LocalStack with AWS environments
- **The Boilers Team (FPT University)** – Minh Hương, Quốc An, and Trí Quân recounted their hackathon journey in an all-English presentation
- **Nguyễn Thị Quỳnh Như (HUTECH University)** – Addressed psychological barriers like self-doubt and strategies for cultivating self-assurance
- **Anh Nghĩa (Swinburne)** – Pitched an innovative startup concept that blends artificial intelligence with astrology
- **Trần Minh Quân (FPT University)** – Analyzed the subtle, often overlooked issues (the "iceberg") in software engineering
- **Khắc Uyên (Vietnam-Germany University - VGU)** – Discussed techniques to combat procrastination and conquer invisible anxieties

### Key Highlights

#### LocalStack for faster testing

- Utilizing LocalStack accelerated the testing cycle by roughly 138 times compared to deploying on actual cloud infrastructure  
- The tool proved to be highly resource-efficient, consuming minimal RAM  
- One drawback noted was its restricted support, covering only a specific subset of AWS services  

#### Hackathon projects

- The Boilers Team showcased their AI-based fake-voice detection system designed to combat call-center fraud  
- Additionally, they developed a platform capable of generating simulated interviews based directly on a user's CV  

#### Self-doubt and Imposter Syndrome

- Feelings of inadequacy and imposter syndrome frequently cause students to pass up valuable opportunities  
- A common trend is for talented individuals to hide their true capabilities simply because they don't feel "ready"  

#### AI and astrology project

- This unique startup idea integrated large language models, specifically Claude, with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)  
- When cross-verified against traditional Bát tự principles, the system demonstrated an exceptionally high rate of accuracy and agreement  

#### Software iceberg problems

- A large portion of software failures are rooted in ambiguous requirements, flawed communication, and overly manual workflows rather than purely technical bugs  
- The most critical threats to a project are usually hidden beneath the surface  

#### Procrastination and fear

- Procrastination often functions as a psychological defense mechanism rather than mere laziness  
- Underlying fears typically include the dread of being judged, fear of failure, and the belief that one lacks sufficient skills  

### Key Takeaways

#### Action creates confidence

- True confidence is built through taking action, not by waiting until you feel perfectly prepared  
- Adopting an Amazon-like "Go Build" mentality promotes rapid execution, early failure, and swift course correction  

#### Use the 5-second rule

- Whenever you feel hesitation creeping in, force yourself to start immediately  
- A helpful tactic is committing to just 5 minutes of focused work on a difficult task to build initial momentum  

#### Join hackathons often

- Hackathons are excellent avenues for accessing free resources, building a network, and working on tangible projects  
- Having real-world projects on a CV carries far more weight than purely theoretical knowledge  

### Event Experience

The session was exceptionally interactive, offering a well-rounded mix of deep technical topics and crucial soft-skills discussions.

#### Strong audience interaction
- Presenters actively engaged the room by posing thought-provoking questions, successfully breaking the ice and fostering dialogue  
- The overall vibe of the event was consistently welcoming and open  

#### Wide topic coverage
- The agenda seamlessly integrated Cloud computing, software architecture, AI, human psychology, and emotional regulation  
- This holistic approach ensured the event was beneficial for both professional skill-building and personal development  

#### Supportive environment
- Even nervous, first-time speakers were met with a high degree of respect and enthusiastic encouragement from the crowd  
- The day concluded on a highly positive note, leaving participants feeling appreciated and inspired
