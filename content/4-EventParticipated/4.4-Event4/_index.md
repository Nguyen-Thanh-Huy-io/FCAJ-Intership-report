# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day 27/06/2026”

### Event Objectives

- Demonstrate tangible AI solutions tailored for enterprise applications
- Illustrate how AWS AI services can streamline operations in customer support, IT operations (DevOps), and human resources
- Highlight the necessity of secure deployments and seamless integration with existing internal architectures

### Speakers

- **Anh Kiệt & Anh Trung (RAI)** – Showcased Voice Agent technologies and the unique hurdles of building call centers for the Vietnamese language
- **Nguyên & Chị Bảo** – Explained how the AWS DevOps AI Agent can be utilized for rapid system diagnostics and repair
- **Trường (Wayne) & Minh Anh (Noventiq)** – Introduced Amazon Q Business as a tool for modernizing recruitment and assessing personnel
- **Toàn Nguyễn (Security Builder) & Anh Nghị (Ra Club)** – Deep-dived into the private security protocols required when linking Amazon Q to internal MCP networks

### Key Highlights

#### Vietnamese Voice AI

- Developing voice systems for Vietnamese is challenging due to limited training data, necessitating meticulous speech processing  
- The underlying architecture strings together Speech-to-Text (STT), an LLM for contextual understanding, and Text-to-Speech (TTS)  
- The resulting model is fine-tuned to manage conversational interruptions, correctly apply cultural honorifics, and understand diverse regional dialects  

#### DevOps automation with AI Agent

- The AWS DevOps AI Agent automatically aggregates system logs and performance traces to swiftly identify the root cause of failures  
- This automation drastically cuts incident resolution times, turning hours or weeks of manual investigation into a task that takes mere minutes  
- Educational and corporate institutions like WGU and KDI have successfully adopted these workflows to accelerate their incident response times  

#### Amazon Q for HR

- Human Resources departments traditionally waste countless hours on the manual screening of resumes  
- Amazon Q automates this by reading CVs, extracting relevant skill sets, mapping them against job descriptions, and assigning candidate scores  

#### Private security with Amazon Q

- To guarantee data privacy, the architecture relies on AWS VPC, Application Load Balancers, and Route 53 Resolvers to confine traffic within a private network  
- This methodology aligns perfectly with Zero Trust principles, ensuring that highly sensitive corporate data is never exposed to the public internet  

### Key Takeaways

#### AI amplifies people, it does not replace them

- While AI Agents are excellent at summarizing data, suggesting actions, and providing preliminary scores, the ultimate decision-making power remains in human hands  
- Human intuition and judgment are still irreplaceable, especially in nuanced fields like DevOps and HR  

#### Foundational data matters

- An AI system is only as good as the data it consumes; it requires comprehensive logs, active alerts, and detailed deployment histories to function well  
- A robust, mature IT infrastructure is a strict prerequisite for generating trustworthy AI insights  

#### Flexible integration is important

- Organizations are not forced into vendor lock-in with a single platform ecosystem  
- Through MCP, Amazon Q offers flexible integration options, connecting easily with SharePoint, Google Workspace, Jira, and custom on-premise servers  

### Event Experience

The overarching theme of the event was extreme practicality, heavily supported by live, interactive demonstrations.

#### Live demos
- Presenters executed real-time demonstrations on environments that closely mirrored actual production setups  
- Attendees had the unique opportunity to watch AI systems automatically respond to simulated DDoS attacks and perform live CV evaluations  

#### Active discussion
- The organizers effectively maintained audience engagement by incorporating mini-games and distributing small prizes  
- The Q&A segments were rich and detailed, with participants probing into technical specifications, operational costs, and the nuances of system behaviors
